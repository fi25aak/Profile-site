Overview

A Subway Surfers-style game where the player runs, jumps over obstacles and collects fruit to gain speed while a dragon chases from behind. The player starts with three lives..

My role and approach

I designed and built this game on my own as part of my college course, handling the player movement, obstacles, fruit collection, speed increase, the chasing dragon and the three-lives system..

Accessibility and usability considerations

I focused on simple controls and clear feedback, such as showing the player's remaining lives and making obstacles and fruit easy to tell apart.

Outcome and reflection

The finished game was playable, with the speed increasing as fruit is collected, a dragon chasing from behind and a lose condition after three lives. I learned how to manage game logic such as collisions, scoring and lives. If I revisited it, I would add clearer instructions and options to make it more accessible.
